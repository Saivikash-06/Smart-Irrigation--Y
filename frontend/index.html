Hardware Simulation Note: In the Wokwi circuit, a rotary potentiometer connected to ESP32 GPIO 34 simulates an analog capacitive soil moisture probe. The ESP32 12-bit ADC converts voltages between 0V and 3.3V into digital integers (0–4095).

Soil Moisture Classification Standards

0% – 29%: DRY

Critically low soil moisture. Irrigation pump activates automatically if system is in Auto Mode.

30% – 44%: ACCEPTABLE

Moisture is adequate but declining. Soil should be continuously monitored.

45% – 75%: COMFORTABLE

Optimal root hydration zone for demonstration plants. Promotes healthy root respiration without saturation.

76% – 100%: HIGH MOISTURE

Soil is saturated. Check plant drainage to prevent root rot or fungal growth.
